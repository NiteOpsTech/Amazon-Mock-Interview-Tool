# Amazon Mock Interviewer Tool (BETA)

This tool is designed to help candidates prepare for technical and behavioral interviews, specifically focusing on Amazon's 16 Leadership Principles. 

### Key Features
* **Behavioral Practice:** Evaluates responses based on Amazon's public Leadership Principles. It highlights a **GREEN FLAG** when you successfully use the STAR method and a **RED FLAG** when key structural elements are missing.
* **Audio & Timer Support:** Integrated microphone recording and an answer timer (plus a few hidden features) to simulate realistic interview conditions.
* **Technical Fundamentals:** Multiple-choice practice covering 25 fundamental questions per topic.
* **L4 Engineering Practice:** A dedicated tab for technicians aiming for L4 Engineering roles. *Note: Data for this section was compiled with AI assistance (Claude) due to limited public sources. If you are a current L4 Engineer, your feedback here is especially valuable!*
* **Reverse Interviewing:** A curated section featuring impactful questions to ask your interview panel, sourced from Google, Reddit, LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor, and tech forums.
